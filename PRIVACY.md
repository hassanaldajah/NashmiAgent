# Privacy Policy for Nashmi Agent

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

Nashmi Agent ("the App", "we", "us") is a cybersecurity-focused AI chat application, built and run by Hassan Aldajah as an independent student project. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Google Sign-In.** When you sign in with Google, we receive only the basic profile information you authorize: your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google data. We never see your Google password.

**Sign-up details.** The first time you sign in you choose a full name and a username, which we store with your account.

**Chat content and project settings.** If you are signed in, your chats (messages and titles), the projects you create, and your preferences are saved to your account so you can come back to them.

**API keys you choose to save.** The App works with your own API key from the AI provider you pick. By default the key is used for your current session only. If you choose to save it to your account, it is stored encrypted.

**Usage and activity.** We keep simple counters of how many requests you make per day, provider, and model; the times you sign in; and a "last seen" time used to show who is online.

**Two-step verification.** If you turn on two-step verification, we store its secret and your backup codes in encrypted form (backup codes as hashes).

**Guests.** If you use the App as a guest, you can only browse it. You cannot send messages, and nothing about you is saved.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To save and show your chats, projects, preferences, and usage.
- To display your name or avatar inside the App.
- To generate AI responses to your messages.
- To keep the service running and protect it from abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

## 3. Third-Party AI Providers

To generate responses, your messages, and any files you attach, are sent to the AI provider you select in the App, using your own API key. The available providers are Google (Gemini), Groq, OpenAI, and Anthropic (Claude). Each provider processes your data under its own privacy policy and terms, and requests to OpenAI and Anthropic are billed to your own account with them. Please do not enter passwords, personal identification numbers, financial details, or other sensitive personal data into the chat.

## 4. Hosting and Service Providers

The App is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. Streamlit may process technical data (such as IP address and browser information) as part of hosting, under its own privacy policy. Account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database run by a third-party hosting provider. Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Signed-in accounts keep their data in a database until you delete it. Chat messages and titles, project instructions, saved API keys, and two-step verification secrets are encrypted before they are stored. Your name, email address, username, and sign-in times are stored as plain account records so the service can identify you.

The person who runs the App administers the database and holds the encryption key, so we do not claim that your data is hidden from the operator. We do not read your chats, and we do not use them for any purpose other than running the App.

You can download a copy of your data or permanently delete your account and everything stored with it from the **Account** page inside the App ("Data and security"). Deleting your account removes your records from the database.

Guests are not stored. Their activity lives only in the browser session.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with anyone except the service providers described above, which are needed for the App to work, or when required by law.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encryption of sensitive fields, per-account access to data, and two-step verification, but no online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## 8. Your Choices

- You can use the App as a guest, or stop using it at any time.
- You can download or delete your data from the Account page.
- You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- To ask about or request deletion of any data we hold about you, contact us at the email below.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
